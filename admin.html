Edit the site

Sign in and every page gets an editing bar along the bottom.

You're in

Open any page and use the bar at the bottom of the screen.

  1. Text — click any heading or paragraph and type over it.
  2. Photos — click a photo to change its size, shape and which part of it shows.
  3. Sections — move whole blocks up and down the page, hide them, or delete them.
  4. Photos — you can also upload a new photo to replace any of them.
  5. Publish — puts everything live for everyone, straight away.

Start editing

Sign out

Changes stay in this browser until you press Publish. Once published they are live for everyone immediately — there is nothing to send on and nothing for anyone else to do.
