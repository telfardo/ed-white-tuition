The practical bits

        
Before you book

      


      

        

          
Mostly online

          
Ed splits his time between England and Bilbao, so lessons are mainly online — which means it doesn't matter where in the world you are.

        

        

          
In person

          
Possible when Ed is nearby. Say where you're based on the form and he'll tell you honestly what's workable.

        

        

          
Lesson length

          
30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes. If none of those fit, pick “open to discussion” and he'll work something out with you.

        

        

          
Who it's for

          
Children, teenagers, adults and retirees. Ed's youngest student is six and his oldest is seventy-five.

        

        

          
No instrument yet?

          
Not a problem — mention it on the form and Ed will point you towards something sensible to hire or buy.

        

        

          
Access needs

          
Ed teaches all ages and learning styles, including neurodivergent learners. Tell him what helps and he'll build the lesson around it.
