How I teach

        
Relaxed, and no hierarchy

        

          Over the years I've had good teachers and awful, awful teachers, and I know exactly
          which kind I'd rather be. We'll get along as equals. Nobody is going to be told off
          in my lesson.
        

        

          I'm easygoing and I'll shape the way I teach around the way you learn. If something
          isn't landing, that's my problem to solve, not yours — we'll come at it from another
          angle until it does.
        

        
I think you learn fastest when you're actually enjoying yourself, so that's what I aim for first.

The practical bits

        
Things you might be wondering

      


      

        

          
Why online?

          
I split my time between England and Bilbao, so online is simply the way I can teach you reliably, week after week, wherever you are.

        

        

          
Can we meet in person?

          
Sometimes, yes — it depends where I am. Tell me where you're based on the form and I'll be honest with you about what's workable.

        

        

          
How long are lessons?

          
30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes. If none of those fit, choose “open to discussion” and we'll sort something out between us.

        

        

          
I haven't got an instrument

          
Not a problem at all. Say so on the form and I'll point you at something sensible to hire or buy before we start.

        

        

          
I'm nervous / I'm too old / I'm hopeless

          
You're not, and I've heard all three many times. Come once and see. There's no contract and nothing to commit to.

        

        

          
I learn differently

          
Good — tell me how. I've taught every age and every sort of learner, including neurodivergent students, and I'll build the lesson around what actually helps you.
