The practical bits

        
Before you book

      


      

        

          
Lesson length

          
30, 45 or 60 minutes. Lessons can be however long you want them to be — say so on the form and Ed will advise.

        

        

          
Where

          
In person in Edinburgh, or online. Both work well; online suits academic tuition particularly.

        

        

          
Who it's for

          
Children, teenagers, adults and retirees. Ed's youngest student is six and his oldest is seventy-five.

        

        

          
No instrument yet?

          
Not a problem — mention it on the form and Ed will point you towards something sensible to hire or buy.

        

        

          
Access needs

          
Ed teaches all ages and learning styles, including neurodivergent learners. Tell him what helps and he'll build the lesson around it.

        

        

          
Questions first?

          
Perfectly normal. Message him on WhatsApp and ask before booking anything.

        

      

    

  


  
  

    


      

        
      


      

        
Who you'll be learning with

        
A teacher who still plays

          and the drums for fifteen. Ten years in orchestras took him on tour around Europe.
        

        

          He now curates world music concerts as a solo artist and co-leads a jazz group in
